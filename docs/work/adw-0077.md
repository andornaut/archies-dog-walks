## Scope

The tip sparkle plays over the lead dog, sized from its texture, for any cash gain that has no animation of its own.

## Criteria

- The sparkle is centred over the lead dog at its texture size.
- A treat that pays cash instead of hearts plays the tip sparkle; a gain with its own animation, such as the wallet, does not.

## Decisions

- Cash gains reuse the tip coin sparkle instead of a new effect.
