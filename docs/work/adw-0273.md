## Scope

Support a full walk and its interface with keyboard alone. Keep sideways-only walk
movement. Give choice cards a shared keyboard and pointer selection outline.

## Criteria

- Arrow keys, WASD and HJKL navigate open interfaces. E, F, Enter and Space activate
  the focused action. Right-click activates an available pointer action.
- A/D, H/L and Left/Right steer the active walk. Escape acts once on the top layer's
  available Back, Close or dialogue Skip. A required layer blocks Escape. In the
  uncovered walk Escape opens Pause. Editors and sliders retain their own input.
- Choice cards start without an outline. The first direction highlights the first card,
  later directions move the highlight, and confirming invokes the existing guarded choice.
- Guards, focus restoration, numbered shortcuts and pointer behaviour are preserved.
- Verify keyboard-only play and pair the source with the controlling Gameplay and Art guides.

## Outcome

Keyboard navigation and sideways steering shipped in 69615bf3. Isolated checks and a browser
walk exercised activation, list and card navigation, dismissal and input guards.
