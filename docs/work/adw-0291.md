## Scope

Use the existing four-logical-pixel icon and label gap for collision heart
notices and related walk notices.

## Criteria

- The heart animation's opaque envelope clears the signed label backing.
- Adjacent visible label backings clear one another by four logical pixels.
- Labels retain their intrinsic padding, shared vertical axis and timing.
- Animating the heart keeps the label group stationary and centred.
