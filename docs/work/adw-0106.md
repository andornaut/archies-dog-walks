## Scope

Review the game rules without simulations, fix the non-controversial discrepancies, and put the rest
to the user in batches.

## Criteria

- Rules match the game where they disagreed, and unclear rules state exactly what happens.
- Each controversial finding has a user decision applied.

## Decisions

- The Fanny pack stays a refund, and its text says so.
- Second chance forgives the owner left with the fewest hearts, ties to the lead dog.
- Stinky, Spotlight, Zoomies and Rush hour keep their values.
- A friendly neighbour's heart that an owner can't take passes to the next owner below their max.
- The walk target is about 9 minutes.
- Legend of the Leash takes 500 walks.
- Badge tiers are rebalanced to 13 easy, 14 medium and 13 hard.
- Card option names are 1 to 4 words.
- The planner's terms move to `docs/planner-work.md`; the design keeps the player-facing spawning
  rules.
- Back on the fork card's End the walk? question returns to the fork card.
