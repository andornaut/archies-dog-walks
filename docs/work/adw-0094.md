## Scope

Known progress fills then disappears, unknown progress shows a travelling segment, and only loads the player waits for show a bar.

## Criteria

- Every bar fades out after its full hold.
- The offline line shows only for a first visit's precache.
- Progress is measured by settled files, and failed multi-file loads retry whole.

## Decisions

- Background loads (service-worker registration, update downloads) show no bar.
