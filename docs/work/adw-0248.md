## Scope

Apply Art's review of the deployed button states: focus and press show as fill, text or icon
changes, never as an outline, ring or movement alone. Choice cards, Logbook rows, Settings rows,
the Pet shop board, the top-bar icons and the shared focus style follow it, including forced
colours. Cheats takes the standard Large panel width.

## Criteria

- No control shows an outline or ring for focus, press or selection; each shows a fill, text or
  icon cue, movement only adding to one; forced colours use Highlight and HighlightText.
- Focused and pressed text keeps at least 4.5:1 contrast; Cheats is 328 wide with 16 px margins.

## Decisions

- Choice options take `outline: none` and show focus as their label band in cyan; an enabled
  card's press keeps the green band. A disabled card shows the cyan band on focus and no press
  band. Forced colours use Highlight and HighlightText.
- Logbook rows keep their content at full opacity under focus and press, and a locked badge's
  goal and progress turn neutralDarkSlate with its name while focused or pressed, so both hold
  5.7:1 on the focus fill; forced colours use HighlightText.
- Settings rows draw no ring. Every row (switch or slider) fills cyan on focus, and a switch row
  fills green with ink text while pressed, as a Pet shop row does, keeping its dip. Cheats'
  switch row takes the same cues.
- Settings shows Cheats' scrollbar while its rows overflow, in the panel's right padding 4 px
  past the rows (`MODAL.scrollGap`), so the rows keep their 296 px width.
- The top-bar faces (Sound, Menu, Pause, Exit) turn from navy to ink under their highlight row
  while pressed; forced colours paint the whole face under the glyph, ButtonFace normally and
  Highlight while focused or pressed, the glyph at full opacity, the artwork rule outranking
  Kit's focused artwork opacity.
- An effect well takes a pressed button's fill while a press on it is a hold candidate: its
  interior inside its frame fills forest, the ink frame separating the wells, and it dips 2 px
  with its icon. The canvas tap burst draws under the DOM walk bar, so the well itself carries
  the cue.
- The Pet shop board alone shows its press; the building stays up, and the board's `pressed`
  frame is forest. The slot sign's pressed face is forest with both lines white and no focus
  fill over it, its code-drawn fallback forest too.
- Every Pet shop row shows focus as the cyan face fill, an unaffordable one included; that row
  keeps its name and line in neutralDarkSlate (5.73:1) under focus, turns them ink (8.41:1) on
  the green press fill as an affordable row does, and keeps its price plate.
- A pressed standard button fills forest under white text (7.80:1). A pressed primary
  ui-button is forest with a white label, on the canvas and in the DOM.
- The shared ring rules are gone; the speech bubble, the last control that used one, fills its
  inside cyan on focus with its owner's name in plum.
- A disabled or refusing standard button does not dip.
- Cheats is the Large panel: 328 wide with 16 px margins, two 144 px columns 8 px apart in the
  296 px body, and its scrollbar in the right padding. Every label fits at full size; Yonge
  Street and Unlock Dog Slots wrap onto two lines, split where the widest line is narrowest
  ("Unlock / Dog Slots").
- A Settings switch's state word ends its ink on the switch's last column (x = 319).
- The ring code is retired: the shop focus frame, the canvas focus ring, the keyboard ring's
  outset, the `ui-select` and `ui-shop-focus` slices and sprites, a selected tile's `ui-select`
  outset and the code-drawn selection frame of the unused canvas tab.
