## Scope

Apply Art's review of the deployed button states: focus and press show as fill, text or icon
changes, never as an outline, ring or movement alone. Choice cards, Logbook rows, Settings rows,
the Pet shop board, the top-bar icons and the shared focus style follow it, including forced
colours. Cheats takes the standard Large panel width.

## Criteria

- No control shows an outline or ring for focus, press or selection; each shows a fill, text or
  icon cue, movement only adding to one; forced colours use Highlight and HighlightText.
- Focused and pressed text keeps at least 4.5:1 contrast; Cheats is 328 wide with 16 px margins.
