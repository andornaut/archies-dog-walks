## Scope

The End the walk? question always shows how much of the cash ending keeps, in the pause menu note's
wording, and what ending gives up.

## Criteria

- The pause menu's note and the question show "Ending now keeps $45 of $70", or "Ending now keeps
  all $70" when ending keeps it all.
- When ending gives anything up, a red line under the question shows what: the current
  neighbourhood's earnings (none at a fork), then the number of neighbourhoods left after this one.

## Decisions

- Show both the keep note and the loss line.
