## Scope

Bitmap fonts for body and title text, a display face from the sign lettering and a comic face for
hit words.

## Criteria

- Body text reads at 1x: capitals and digits six rows tall with a drawn lower case.
- Glyphs that misread at 1x ($, 9, %) are redrawn.

## Decisions

- Body text keeps Tiny5 and titles keep Silkscreen, with Art's own glyph fixes.
