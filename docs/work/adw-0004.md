## Scope

Road sprites, faces and 64x64 portraits for the nine dogs, with Archie the Australian Shepherd as
the starter. Each dog has a walk cycle, a breathing idle, react, pull and tell frames, a shake-off
and its own poses, drawn in small, medium and large size classes.

## Criteria

- Each dog reads on every neighbourhood road at every depth step.
- Faces and portraits match the road sprite's markings, and the size classes step evenly.

## Decisions

- Archie is the starter and mascot, drawn as a brown and white Australian Shepherd.
- Big dogs are drawn in a 54 px frame and narrowed so a trio's lead stays readable.
