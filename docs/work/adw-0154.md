## Scope

Pair the rules and the Art guides with shipped behaviour: owner-led message lines at full size,
confirmations without a redundant labelled Close footer, the primary action last or rightmost, a tap
action as the default hold action, and no mute toggle on the Boot, loading and Retry views.

## Criteria

- Update, End the walk?, Purchase and Quit confirmations show only their semantic choices; their X,
  Escape, labels, effects and guards remain.
- The primary action is last in a vertical group and rightmost in a horizontal row.
- A click or tap action is also the default hold action: it fires once, with no second action on
  release. An explicitly different hold action keeps its behaviour.
- Owner-led message strip lines keep every measured row at full size.
- Boot, loading and Retry views have no mute toggle.

## Decisions

- Remove the redundant labelled Close footers consistently.
- The primary-action placement and the default hold action follow the shared game policies.
