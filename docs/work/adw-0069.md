## Scope

Every runtime spawn plan places all of its requested spawns within the planner's bounded search. The
target is reached by improving the planner, with the neighbourhoods' configured spawn rates kept.

## Criteria

- Planner acceptance (six fixed seeds, each on the left, straight and right routes: 18 walks and 72
  plans) completes every plan.
- Every plan completes within 75% of the per-attempt search allowance.
- Guaranteed owners, kind and role minima, full movement capabilities, native safety and quiet
  coverage are never traded for delivery.
- The independently certified 90% floor remains the release rule.

## Decisions

- Improve the planner instead of lowering spawn rates or thinning Yonge Street's minor movers.
- Acceptance requires 100% placement within 75% of the per-attempt allowance; release keeps the 90%
  certified floor.
