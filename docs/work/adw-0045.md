## Scope

Card obstacles, minor obstacles and lane-spanning statics for each Toronto neighbourhood, with their
hit bursts, hit sounds and option cards, and the street cart skins.

## Criteria

- No obstacle reads as the tip coin.
- A static may stand taller where the height makes its silhouette read.

## Decisions

- The Distillery District's sculpture became closed shipping crates, and the fish crates became the
  green bin spill.
