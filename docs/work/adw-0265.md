## Scope

Investigate the frequently appearing small animal in High Park, make roadside movement and
arrival timing less predictable, and review spawn rates across neighbourhoods. Clear stale
visuals when a fork or live neighbourhood replacement keeps the same run index.

## Criteria

- A neighbourhood replacement cannot display the old neighbourhood's queued movers or scenery.
- Roadside timings vary across their safe windows, and moving animals remain visible in motion.
- Record comparable before-and-after rates by neighbourhood. Preserve planner safety, quotas
  and guarantees unless the user explicitly changes a rule.

## Decisions

- High Park has no authored cat. Cat art from an earlier neighbourhood must clear on replacement.

## Outcome

Forks and live neighbourhood replacements discard stale visuals. Safe distraction starts
vary across their feasible windows, with room for clearance retries. The decorative target
is 17 per neighbourhood and visits linger for a quarter of their visible lifetime. The
six paired fixtures showed 126 to 101 visible starts and longer visible motion for visits.
Real hazards and their guarantees keep their existing rates.
