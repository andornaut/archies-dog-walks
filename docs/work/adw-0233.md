## Scope

Keep entry through the slot sign and other Start targets, and retain refusal
feedback and hold-to-inspect without selecting disabled rows.

The Shop keeps unaffordable dogs, slots and gear unselected on tap, keyboard
navigation and entry from Start. A browser check confirmed that the slot sign
opens the Dogs tab without a selection and that tapping the slot retains its
grey appearance and shortfall message. Shipped in `c5c2b258`.
