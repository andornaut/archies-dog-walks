## Scope

A read-only audit of the whole source: a dependency matrix of every source directory, type-only
imports included; the layers the code forms; every import cycle and upward or sideways import;
internals imported from outside their area's public entry; unused exports; the largest files and
their seams; decisions tangled with effects (DOM, storage, clock, randomness, engine objects) and
module-level caches; duplicated helpers and dead code; and where tests, fixtures, simulators and
tools sit relative to shipped code. It proposes named layers with imports pointing down only, one
public entry per area, an import check that fails on any cycle or wrong-direction import, pure
functions with effects at the edges and no test-only production paths, as ranked steps that each
preserve behaviour and land on their own. Building any step is a separate item the user approves.

## Criteria

- The audit changes no code.
- Each proposed step states its scope, its behaviour proof and its conflicts with work in flight.

## Decisions

- The user approved all eleven proposed steps as adw-0259 (2026-11-25).

## Outcome

572 production modules form twelve layers, and 3,464 of 3,493 production imports already point
down. No value-import cycles; 11 file-level cycles counting type-only imports; 29 upward and 1
sideways import. Only `data` and `pwa` have a public entry. Report, matrix and scripts:
`game-coordinator/var/handoffs/games/archies-dog-walks/tech/proofs/adw-0258/`.
