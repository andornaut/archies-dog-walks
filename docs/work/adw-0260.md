## Scope

The walker's speech bubble on the Start screen sets its text 2 clear rows below the bubble's top
and none above its bottom. Centre the visible ink of the whole text block vertically in the
bubble's cream body, excluding the tail, for every line count the bubble shows.

## Criteria

- The clear rows above and below the text's ink differ by at most one, the odd row below, for one,
  two and three lines.
- Horizontal placement, wrapping and the bubble's size are unchanged unless centring needs a taller
  body, which Art reviews.
- Before and after captures go to Art.

## Decisions

- A bubble with a name pill on its first or last line grows 2 rows to keep the pill's 2-row gap
  (Art, 2026-11-25).
- Each line centres horizontally on its visible ink, the spare column on the left (Art,
  2026-11-25).
- Plain bubbles grow 2 rows (top pad 5, bottom pad 4), so a line with ascenders and descenders
  keeps 1 clear row each side (user, 2026-11-25, from Art's capture sheet).
