## Scope

Settings and Cheats open as frameless full-screen pages, like the Pet shop, the Logbook and Help,
instead of modal cards: Game Kit's page frame under the live top bar, a game-placed title, the rows
scrolling in the page body and the page Back at its fixed place. Each returns to the screen it
opened over, from home or from the paused walk, as Help does.

## Criteria

- Settings and Cheats use the shared page template and frame, with no card, border or close
  control.
- Their rows, switches, sliders and refusals behave as they do today.
- Before and after captures go to Art.

## Decisions

- A page sits at the same level as the top bar and the rest of the screen, and a modal sits on
  top of it, so only a modal closes on a tap outside it. No page closes on a tap on the top bar,
  the walk bar or beside the game; pages leave by their page Back, Escape or the system back, and
  the top bar's controls keep working over them (user, 2026-11-25).

## Outcome

Settings and Cheats are frameless pages on the shared page template under the live top bar, with
Art's page geometry: rows across the content column, a fixed title, version label and Back, and the
Cheats grid in two 157-wide columns. No page closes on an outside tap; pages leave by their Back,
Escape or the system back, and only modals close on a tap outside them. Every Settings and Cheats
refusal plays the shared cue, and in forced colours separators draw in CanvasText, refusals invert
on buttons, rows and choice cards, and picker tiles and Shop rows refuse in Highlight. Art accepted
every part on 2026-11-25.
