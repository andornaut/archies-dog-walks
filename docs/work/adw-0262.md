## Scope

Settings and Cheats open as frameless full-screen pages, like the Pet shop, the Logbook and Help,
instead of modal cards: Game Kit's page frame under the live top bar, a game-placed title, the rows
scrolling in the page body and the page Back at its fixed place. Each returns to the screen it
opened over, from home or from the paused walk, as Help does.

## Criteria

- Settings and Cheats use the shared page template and frame, with no card, border or close
  control.
- Their rows, switches, sliders and refusals behave as they do today.
- Before and after captures go to Art.
