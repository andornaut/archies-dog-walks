## Scope

Use a direct blue selection treatment for selectable interface controls,
including the Logbook, Settings and Pet shop.

## Criteria

- Selection uses blue directly, without an intervening green fill.
- Selection does not fade, pulse or move the control.
- Pointer and keyboard selection share the same appearance.
- Preserve current activation, eligibility, refusal and accessible state.
