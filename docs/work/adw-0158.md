## Scope

Under the full-background modal rule (`61dd24189`), the rules treat the Pet shop and the Logbook as
pages, not modals.

## Criteria

- While either page is open, the home top bar stays live and undimmed.
- A card over either page (a detail card, the purchase confirm or the Logbook's badge card) is a
  modal and dims everything behind it, the top bar included.

## Decisions

- The Logbook is a page like the Pet shop.
