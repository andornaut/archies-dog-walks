## Scope

On Settings and Cheats switch rows the state word (ON, OFF) sits right-aligned to the row's right
inset while the switch above it sits 8 in, so the two do not share a centre. Centre the state
word's ink horizontally on the switch's centre, on every switch row.

## Criteria

- Each switch row's state word and switch share one vertical centre line.
- Before and after captures go to Art.

## Decisions

- Every switch moves 7 px left to 291-323, 15 in from the row's right, so the widest state word
  centred on it ends on the content edge (user via Art, 2026-11-25).

## Outcome

Every Settings and Cheats switch sits at 291 to 323 with its state word ink-centred on 307; slider
values stay right-aligned to 330. Art accepted it on 2026-11-25.
