## Scope

Since the dog faces fill their frames, a walk dog card's owner name no longer fits its column and
breaks inside a word. The card gives the name the room it needs instead.

## Criteria

- No owner name breaks inside a word on a walk dog card or the results page; text keeps its full
  size, and every owner name keeps its wording.
- The card stays 120x70; the hearts, dog photo, LEAD tag, walk HUD height and road view keep
  their places.

## Decisions

- Name under the photo (user, 2026-11-18, option A of Art's mocks): the owner photo moves up 5
  rows, and a name that fits 71 px wide sits on one line under it; a longer name goes beside the
  photo in two lines that wrap only at spaces.
- On the results page a word wider than the owner column ends at the well's right edge, under
  the bubble, with no taller row.

## Outcome

Shipped in 9aa3f6e9e and 61fda5347: every owner name fits whole at full size, Gadget
included, and the owner name layout fails a build rather than split a word.
