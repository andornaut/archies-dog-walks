## Scope

Under a modal, every region outside it, top controls included, is painted, dimmed and inactive through game-kit's strict dialog isolation.

## Criteria

- A background press runs the existing Cancel or Back once when the modal is closeable.
- Mandatory choices stay non-dismissible.

## Decisions

- Pages (the Pet shop and Logbook) keep a live top bar; cards over them dim everything.
