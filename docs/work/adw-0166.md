## Scope

Start screen fixes the user asked Art for: picker portraits, dog cards, section header bars, the
TORONTO sign, the top-right buttons and button label centring.

## Criteria

- Picker portraits (dogs, walkers, gear) fill their boxes, as the dog portrait did before.
- Unbought dog cards pad the name and price as the portrait is padded.
- Bought dog cards centre a larger name in the space right of the portrait, so the longest name
  fits on one line.
- Each section header bar's right note is padded as its title is on the left, the same in all
  three bars.
- The TORONTO sign is larger.
- The top-right buttons have no dark square backing and are centred vertically in the bar.
- Every button label, Start walk included, is centred vertically by its ink.

## Decisions

- Every dog face is drawn as large as its frame holds whole, in the pickers and on the walk's dog
  cards alike, and the face size classes are dropped (user, 2026-11-18).
- Dog names stay at 2x, the largest that fits Sequoia on one line: a bought dog's name is centred
  by its ink beside the portrait, and an unbought dog's name and price keep the portrait's padding
  (user, 2026-11-18).
- Walker and gear pictures stay full size; Art mocks smaller plates so the name or price no
  longer covers their lower third, for a later decision (user, 2026-11-18).
- Section header titles and notes are centred down their band, and the bank well down the top
  bar, as the buttons are (user, 2026-11-18).
