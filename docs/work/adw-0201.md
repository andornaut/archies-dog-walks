## Scope

Item modals from Start and the Pet Shop show Cancel and Buy when the current
save can purchase the item, with Buy primary. When no purchase is available,
show one centred Close action. Use the existing action labels and purchase
rules. One shared modal for dogs, gear and slots shows the item details, price
and balance after purchase without a second confirmation. Centre the price and
its currency mark as a group. On unaffordable cards centre the existing saving
prompt on its own row. Separate the price from the owner row with the standard
divider. Slots use their current capacity, with no owner. Walkers remain
information only.

Use the approved dog portrait and owner arrangement and shared section gaps.
Recheck affordability and ownership when Buy is invoked. Preserve dismissal,
focus return and the consequences of a completed purchase.
