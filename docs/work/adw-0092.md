## Scope

Departures use their own load limit of 30, separate from the limit of 20 for regular work, and wait for the load to fall instead of skipping the hour.

## Criteria

- `delivery-kit.json` sets the departure load limit to 30.
- A held departure re-checks the load every minute for up to 15 minutes.

## Decisions

- Two limits: 20 for container work, 30 for departures.
