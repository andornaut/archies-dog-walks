## Scope

During a walk, choosing a neighbourhood in Cheats (opened from Pause) flashes the button, then
shows it selected only after a visible pause. The press selects it at once; the road switch
follows without holding back the selection.

## Criteria

- The pressed neighbourhood shows as selected in the same frame as the press, with no visible
  delay, and the walk still switches to that neighbourhood.

## Decisions

- Cause: the press switched the walk synchronously before redrawing Cheats, and the switch plans
  the new neighbourhood's road, a main-thread task long enough to hold back the frame.
- Fix: the press draws the chosen neighbourhood selected at once and the switch runs in the task
  after that frame paints; another cheat or closing Cheats applies a pending switch first, and a
  second press before the paint replaces it.

## Outcome

Shipped in 6ffc7a38f. The Cheats modal (`src/scenes/cheatsModal.ts`) shows the pressed neighbourhood selected on the
next frame and then switches the walk. Local Chromium at 360x608 measured input to next paint at
32 to 80ms, from 256 to 1344ms before; the road switch that follows still holds the paused frame
for 0.2 to 1.3s with the new selection shown.
