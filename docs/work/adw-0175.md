## Scope

Apply the shared Saves and cheats rules: the Dev panel below the game is retired into a Cheats
modal opened from Settings, with the cheat list Gameplay records.

## Criteria

- Settings shows Open Cheats beside Back, in browser tabs only.
- Cheats is a large scrolling modal with Run-only and Save-changing groups.
- A full Reset save clears the save, badges included.
- An FPS counter toggle shows the rate in the device viewport's bottom right corner, inset from
  the safe area and drawn above everything, letterbox bars included.
- No cheat touches achievements or statistics.

## Decisions

- A walk that uses any gameplay cheat writes nothing to the save, as a replay does: no
  checkpoint, banking, statistics, records or badges. Hit boxes, dev stats and the FPS counter
  only draw and do not count. This supersedes adw-0088's Start at banking (user, 2026-11-18).
- The cheat list and modal layout follow `gameplay/cheats.md`.
