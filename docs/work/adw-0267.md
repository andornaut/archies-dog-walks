## Scope

Measure game startup, normal play and the planner handoff. Implement the highest-value
improvements supported by comparable measurements.

## Criteria

- Record matched before-and-after results and describe the tested environment.
- Preserve gameplay, rendering, accessibility, and offline and update behaviour.
- Run focused checks for the changed contracts and a production build.

## Outcome

The road raster copies cached verge rows and reuses the mixed seed when choosing dot colours.
Across seven paired batches of 1,680 frames at the logical 360 × 308 road size, median raster
CPU time fell from 557.6 ms to 515.9 ms in the isolated Node 24.22.2 harness. All pixels matched
across the six neighbourhoods, including transitions. Controlled Chromium runs preserved
first-zone placement and showed no measurable frame-cadence change.

The first-walk worker prototype reduced the longest post-tap main-thread task from 996 ms to
183 ms, while median time from the tap to the playable walk increased from 1.54 s to 2.90 s.
The production launch path stays on the original implementation.
