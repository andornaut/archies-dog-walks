## Scope

Consolidate the native interface's own-file art into one sheet per asset set with frame data.
Art exports the sheets and their manifest; Tech loads, readies and draws them.

## Criteria

- Each set ships as one sheet within the texture limit verified on mid-range Android.
- The DOM art reads frames from the sheet manifest, readiness is per sheet and the precache
  lists the sheets instead of the own files.
- Rendered pixels are unchanged.

## Decisions

- The six sets pack with art-kit `packAtlas` at padding 1 into lossless WebP sheets of at most
  2048 px, each with a slim frame JSON; a copy shares its base sheet's rects.
- The crash screen's art is inlined, so it never waits on a sheet.

## Outcome

Shipped in 269a599b3, a39b417cb, 99bc2fafb and 773cb511a, with Art's gallery reading the sheets: the
first install drops from 792 files and 7.21 MB to 89 files and 6.78 MB, the interface art from 716
files to 14, with identical pixels. The End screen's new-best ribbon, missing from every build, now
draws, and the unused dimmed copies are gone.
