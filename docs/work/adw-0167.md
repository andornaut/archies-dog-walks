## Scope

Help opens as a page, like the Pet shop and the Logbook, from the Start screen and from Pause in
a walk. Its pictures get a distinct framed treatment so a pictured top bar cannot be mistaken for
the real one.

## Criteria

- On the Start screen, Pet shop, Logbook and Help are pages and Settings is the only screen that
  opens as a modal; the Menu and the small confirmations and cards stay modals.
- Help draws no top bar of its own: the real top bar stays live and undimmed while it is open.
- Help opened from Pause is a page over the paused walk, and closing it returns to Pause.
- Help's pictures use the framed treatment the user picks from Art's mock-ups.

## Decisions

- Help becomes a page; Settings stays a modal (user, 2026-11-18).
- Art mocks a smaller, bordered, captioned picture treatment, with alternatives, before it is
  built (user, 2026-11-18).
- Help from Pause is also a page (user, 2026-11-18).
