## Scope

A focused item for sale shows its price when the bank covers it, and otherwise only how much more
the player needs.

## Criteria

- An affordable item shows "{price}: tap to buy."
- An unaffordable item shows "Need {amount} more.", where the amount is the price less the bank.

## Decisions

- The shortfall line names only the amount, not the price.

## Outcome

String `0890bb992` and Tech source `b56da6684`. The lines live in `src/data/homeText.ts`
(`focus.priceBuy` and `focus.priceShort`).
