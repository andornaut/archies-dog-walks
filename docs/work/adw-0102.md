## Scope

On the start screen, a notice (unsaved progress or a pending update) shows in the walker's bubble in
place of the message it would stack on.

## Criteria

- A notice shows alone in the bubble.
- Notices take at most 2 lines.
- The first-visit greeting fits the bubble on 2 lines.

## Decisions

- A notice shows alone, at 2 lines at most, so the bubble keeps clear of the walker.

## Outcome

[Home screen and progression](../design/gameplay/design.md#home-screen-and-progression) (Walker's
bubble) and the line lengths in [Voice](../design/gameplay/design.md#voice) (Lengths).
