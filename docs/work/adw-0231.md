## Scope

Use the existing updater on entry to play as well as launch, a genuine return
to Start and foreground return. Preserve quiet staging, automatic activation
of a verified stage at cold launch, and the existing Update now and Cancel
behaviour during play. Verify the transition with and without first-walk Help.

## Decisions

- D1 (user, 2026-11-27): Check for updates only on existing events, including startup, resume, return to Start and entry to play. Offer optional updates through **Update now** during an open session. Activate a verified staged update on cold launch. Shared decision with `ada-0413/D1` and `gk-0028/D2`.

## Outcome

A live browser verified one shared update check when play began, both after
first-walk Help and after a direct Start. The service worker stayed in control
and the document did not navigate. The exact change shipped in `c5c2b258`.
