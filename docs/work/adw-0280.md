## Scope

Centre the visible effect wells on the screen when the group fits between the
currency well and the right edge of the reserved region.

## Criteria

- One through five effect wells centre on the screen.
- Six wells centre within the reserved region with clearance on both sides.
- Currency retains its five-character capacity and controls retain their geometry.
