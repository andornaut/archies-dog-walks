## Scope

Ordinary modals use Large (328) or Medium (264) width with a 16 logical px margin; height follows content and fonts stay full size.

## Criteria

- Large: Settings, rich details, lists and install cards. Medium: Pause, Menu, brief confirmations, Update and Purchase.
- Shop rows size from their wrapped content.

## Decisions

- A short detail uses Medium only when its full body fits, otherwise Large.
- Help keeps its full-page frame.
