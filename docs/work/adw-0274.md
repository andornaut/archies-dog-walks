## Scope

Use readable text roles for action labels, choice titles and costs, and the
neighbourhood-entry banner. Align the visible ink with its face. Match the walk
notice leads to the established effect icons and owner portraits. Remove the
visible Sound caption and owner-name caption below the notice portrait.

## Criteria

- Ordinary action labels are larger and their ink is centred without changing how
  the controls act.
- Choice cards are taller, with full readable titles, a centred cost plaque and
  space for the illustration and outcome. Existing choice safeguards remain.
- Both neighbourhood-entry banner lines grow within their existing faces.
- Walk notices display full-size lead icons or portraits with their existing text.
- Sound remains identifiable by its icon and accessible state without the caption.
- Single-column Settings rows centre each painted switch above its state and
  use less vertical space while preserving their targets and sliders.
- Verify the refreshed live Help examples and save screenshots after the layout
  fixes are complete. The normal Help layout shows all text and controls without
  scrolling, with an overflow path for exceptional fits.

## Decisions

- D1 (user, 2026-11-27; answers q-archie-notice-inline-owner): Show owner names alone inside notice sentences. Keep the full-size portrait at the start when the owner is the speaker.
