installed apps. Return one layer and restore invoking focus through either
the top-right X or centred Back. Keep action eligibility, saving and reset
guards, and URL tools.

measured height and body scrolling as needed. Put Next Walk first, then
Start Screen with Unlocks and Money in two columns. Keep action labels at
their full size and offer the three approved money amounts.

nonoverlapping 48-pixel hits and move the visible rows 6 pixels towards
one another, leaving their centres 36 pixels apart. Under Start At, offer
all six neighbourhoods in game order and show the current selection.

Remove Movement Speed, Pocket Item, Seed and Copy Run Link from the modal.
Keep the underlying URL tools and the other existing actions. Move the
existing FPS Counter without changing its device-setting behaviour.
