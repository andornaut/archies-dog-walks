off its last line with no sign that it scrolls, and Help over the paused walk blanks the walk
bar's centre slot.

## Criteria

- Settings over Help dims all of Help, as the Menu over Help does.
- The body shows whole lines and makes its scrolling visible, as Art specifies.
- Help over the paused walk leaves the walk bar as the pause screen shows it.
