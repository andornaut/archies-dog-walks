## Scope

Defects found in the shipped Help page: Settings over Help dims only part of it, the body cuts
off its last line with no sign that it scrolls, and Help over the paused walk blanks the walk
bar's centre slot.

## Criteria

- Settings over Help dims all of Help, as the Menu over Help does.
- The body shows whole lines and makes its scrolling visible, as Art specifies.
- Help over the paused walk leaves the walk bar as the pause screen shows it.

## Decisions

- Settings draws after Help, so Settings over Help dims all of it as the Menu does.
- The walk bar's effect icons draw on the bar's own layer, which stays live over Help, so they
  show undimmed there.
- The body shows three whole 18 px lines, scrolls by whole lines and shows the house scrollbar only
  when it overflows. Text runs x 8..337 and the track x 344..351, inside the frame's 8 px margins
  (Art, 2026-11-18).
