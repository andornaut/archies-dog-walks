## Scope

Set canvas text sizes by role, with full-scene hero typography as its own role.

## Criteria

- Text that does not fit grows its box or shortens its words; it never steps the font down.
- A label's ink sits 10 rows above its help text's capitals.

## Decisions

- The typography roles in the art guides are authoritative.
