## Scope

Every discrete click or tap control fires its action once on a hold as well, through the shared binding, unless explicitly overridden.

## Criteria

- The release after a hold clicks nothing.
- Foreground, modal and readiness changes cancel a pending hold.

## Decisions

- Continuous, drag and native editing controls are explicit exceptions.
