## Scope

Pickups, obstacles, encounters, lucky encounters, forced stops, need spots, scenery gags, moving
hazards and zone skins for the first Maplewood zones.

## Criteria

- Every road sprite writes its feet row and opaque width to the manifest.
- The tip, treat and wallet pickups read apart at road size.

## Decisions

- Art for cut encounters, needs, skins and gags is retired rather than kept unplaced.
- The treat is a white bone with a red heart so it reads on light and dark roads.
