## Scope

The four walkers (Ada, Casey, Mr. B and Trista) seen from behind on the road, with picker portraits,
a front wave tag for the unlock stop, a cheer tag, 64x64 head-and-shoulders busts and Mr. B's phone
pose for his welcome.

## Criteria

- Back-view arms hang from a fixed shoulder and swing against the legs.
- The head, neck and shoulders sway as one, so the neck stays on the collar.

## Decisions

- Each walker wears a different top so the four read apart at a glance.
