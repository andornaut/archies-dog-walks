The walk top bar holds Sound and Pause in fixed slots like home's Sound and Menu; Help moves into the pause menu, and effect icons grow and centre.

## Criteria

- Visible effects centre within the fixed region between the cash well and the controls.
- Effect icons use dedicated 24 px art.
- Cash has inner padding in its well.

## Decisions

- Effects centre by count of visible effects, not from the first slot.
