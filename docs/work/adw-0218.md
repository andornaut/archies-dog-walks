## Scope

Keep Reset Save visible in its current menu. Disable it without stored
progress and after reset. Keep confirmation and one-layer cancellation.
Preserve device settings and ignore cheat-only progress when deciding
availability.

## Decisions

None yet.

## Outcome

Reset Save is disabled without saved progress and after reset. Browser
checks confirmed that cancellation preserves the save and restores focus,
while confirmation clears progress and earned badges, preserves device
settings and reloads once. The scheduled gate and CI passed before deployment.
