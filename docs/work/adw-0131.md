## Scope

Modals take a Large (328) or Medium (264) logical width with 16 px outer margins, content-driven
heights and full-size fonts, assigned per surface.

## Criteria

- Settings, rich details, lists and walkthroughs are Large; Pause, Menu, confirmations, updates and
  purchases are Medium.
- Footers with a sole Close centre it; Help keeps its full-page frame.

## Decisions

- The user approved the width table and then the surface map.
- Celebration panels and the loading panel take a width family too.
