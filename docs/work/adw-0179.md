## Scope

Help, the Logbook and the Pet shop fill the screen: no top bar and no frame or border around the
page. Each page leaves by its own Back button.

## Criteria

- Help, the Logbook and the Pet shop show no top bar and no border, and keep full-size text.
- Each page's leave control reads Back and returns where the page was opened from; the page
  offers no Menu or Sound control of its own.

## Decisions

- Back on every page, Help included (user, 2026-11-19).
- The Pet shop goes full screen with Help and the Logbook (user, 2026-11-19).
- A page offers nothing besides its Back button: no Menu or Sound control (user, 2026-11-19).
