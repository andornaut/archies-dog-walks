## Scope

Help, the Logbook and the Pet shop run edge to edge under the live top bar, with no frame, border
or margin, so no page looks like a modal. Each page leaves by its own Back button.

## Criteria

- Help, the Logbook and the Pet shop keep the live top bar and show no frame, border or margin,
  and keep full-size text.
- Each page's leave control reads Back and returns where the page was opened from.

## Decisions

- The pages keep the top bar and lose only their frame and margin, replacing an earlier full-screen
  choice before any of it was built (user, 2026-11-19).
- Back on every page, Help included (user, 2026-11-19).
- The Pet shop is drawn like Help and the Logbook (user, 2026-11-19).
- The Pet shop and Logbook pages are cream to the screen edges, and Help stays on its brown page
  (user, 2026-11-19, Art's mocks).
