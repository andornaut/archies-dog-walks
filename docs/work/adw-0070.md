## Scope

When lines queue on the walk's message strip, badge lines wait for their turn instead of being
dropped.

## Criteria

- A badge line is never dropped, only delayed.
- Hints and the firing line follow the same rule.
- Any other line still waiting after about 3 seconds, or the oldest such line when too many are
  waiting, is dropped.

## Decisions

- Badge lines are never dropped; hints and the firing line share the rule.
