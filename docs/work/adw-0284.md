## Scope

Use the shared game-homepage structure while preserving Archie's visual identity
and existing site functionality.

## Criteria

- Lead with a themed hero containing the title, a concise premise, primary Play
  and the existing Install action where available.
- Follow with one compact About or Highlights section containing a short gameplay
  summary or up to three brief highlights, then Controls and a compact footer.
- Keep sections aligned to one responsive content column in the same reading order.
- Preserve branding, assets, metadata, navigation and installation behaviour.

## Outcome

Shipped in `864014145`. The live homepage and Play entry matched the checked
artifact. Play and Install fit the hero at 320px and 1200px, with their original
actions verified in a browser preview. The preview used fallback fonts.
