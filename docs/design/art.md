# Art

The game's pixel art, effects and sound are drawn by script. The generators in `art/tools/` are
the source and the files in `public/art/` are their deterministic exports, so a diff after a
build is a real change. Art owns asset keys and geometry, the palette, lights, roadside and verge
data, the app icons and the visual rules every screen follows: one chrome for panels and
controls, contrast floors, full-size fonts, the modal width families and uniform modal dimming.
A visual change ships with before-and-after media reviewed by Art.

Reference: [art README](../../art/README.md), [style guide](../../art/style-guide.md),
[controls](../../art/controls.md), [layout](../../art/layout.md),
[inventory](../../art/inventory.md).
