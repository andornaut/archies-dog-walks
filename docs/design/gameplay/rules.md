# Rules

Controls, screen behaviour and the walk lifecycle. See [Layout](../../../art/layout.md) for interface measurements and [Art controls](../../../art/controls.md) for visual roles.

## Screen and controls

- **Screen** fits the viewport and sits at the top below the safe area, with spare height below the game. In landscape it turns with a phone or tablet and stays upright on a computer with a mouse. Archie is designed for touch and keyboard on a phone browser. See [Coordinates and scale](../../../art/layout.md#coordinates-and-scale).
- **Road** uses a three-quarter view and five lanes, each 64 px wide. The lanes converge toward a hard edge at the backdrop, and objects shrink with distance. Perspective leaves lane distances and collisions unchanged. About 3.6 seconds of road is visible at High Park's speed.
- **Walk HUD** shows cash, effect wells, Sound and Pause or Menu in its top bar. Effect wells fill left to right with the walker perk, Pocket item, found gear and up to three upgrades, with no empty reservations. The neighbourhood pill names the neighbourhood and its place in the walk ("2 of 4"). It sits below the bottom debuff bar or the top bar when no debuff is active. The message strip overlays the backdrop, the debuff bars and the pill. The road scrolls beneath the backdrop's edge. One doghouse card per owned slot sits above the neighbourhood progress line, bordered like the debuff bars. The line fills from left to right and resets in each neighbourhood. See [HUD and overlays](../../../art/layout.md#hudoverlays).
- **Doghouse cards** appear in pack order: lead, left and right. Each shows the dog's name and face, the owner's face and hearts, and the full owner name on one line below the photo or on two lines beside it, broken at a space. The lead card has a LEAD tag. A fired dog's card keeps the fired owner's face. Heart changes and firings show the owner in a photo tab. The firing moment carries its own quip and other quips go in the message strip. Hold a card still to see its dog's size, hearts and owner. See [HUD and overlays](../../../art/layout.md#hudoverlays).
- **Hold to explain:** during a walk, holding anything that explains itself (an effect icon, a debuff bar, a dog card), or tapping an effect icon, opens its explanation in a detail card, the same one the home screen's Details show, never in the message strip, and pauses the walk. The card stays up after the finger lifts; a tap anywhere closes it and resumes the walk. A tap that dismisses the detail card does not activate a background walk bar button or any other underlying action
- **Drag** (the main touch control): put a thumb down anywhere and drag. The first lane step comes after a quarter of a lane-width of travel, and the Nth at N lane-widths from where the drag started, so the thumb never covers the walker. Dragging back a quarter of a lane-width from the furthest point turns the drag around, counting again from there. The pack moves lane by lane toward the target at the walker's lane-change speed
- **Tap:** moves one lane toward the tapped half of the road, as soon as the finger lands; on the doghouse row, when it lifts, so holding a card only explains it. A drag shorter than the first lane step counts as a tap, and a drag whose first step goes against the tap takes the tap back, so the pack ends where the drag alone puts it
- **Keyboard steering** uses Left/Right, A/D or H/L during active walking. Each press moves one lane. Holding a key does not repeat.
- While moving between lanes, the pack can hit things in either lane. A reverse press turns the pack around at once; each other press queues one more lane
- Taps on the top bar never move the pack; taps and drags on the doghouse row steer like anywhere on the road. During a walk, every part of the screen below the top bar steers, the margins around the game included: a drag or tap there acts as one at the nearest edge of the game area, and the top bar and the margin beside or above it keep their own input
- **Buttons** use one horizontal row or vertical column per action group. Vertical buttons share the widest action's width. The primary action is bottommost or rightmost. Where grown targets overlap, the nearest centre wins. Debuff bars and walk effect wells keep their compact target exceptions. A visible debuff bar takes input priority over the walk bar where their targets overlap. Illustrated choice cards, doghouse cards and end-screen badges keep their own spacing. Primary buttons are green, secondary cream and icon buttons navy. Pressed buttons move down and disabled buttons are grey. Refused controls briefly shake and change their fill, including Start, locked or pack-full home picks, unaffordable card choices, disabled Previous in Help and muted volume sliders. The usual cue is a dark red face with a white label and icon. Reduced motion holds the refusal fill steady. Forced colours retain a distinct refusal fill. Grey shop rows keep their actions. Text stays full size. A 500 ms hold performs the default click or tap action once unless the control has an explicit hold action, such as details or enlargement. Its release does nothing further. Steering, dragging, native text editing and hold-only debuff bars keep their own rules. See [Controls and targets](../../../art/layout.md#controlstargets).

## Navigation

Shared [product consistency rules](https://github.com/andornaut/game-coordinator/blob/main/docs/design/game-policies.md#product-consistency) apply alongside Archie's controls and layout in this guide.

- **Keyboard navigation** uses the arrow keys, W/A/S/D or H/J/K/L to move focus or selection through interface controls. E, F, Enter and Space activate the focused or selected available action in the foreground interface once per intentional press. Enter or Space on a focused, available switch toggles it once. Native text editing, selection and slider keys keep their usual behaviour. See [Choice cards](encounters.md#choice-cards) for their selection rules.
- **Right-click** activates an available foreground action under the pointer. Otherwise the browser keeps its native context behaviour. Existing left-click and input safeguards still apply.
- **Escape** belongs to the topmost foreground surface, including before it draws. Once the surface accepts input, one fresh press uses its visible, available Back, Close or X to dismiss one layer when dismissal is allowed. A held key’s repeats and presses that the foreground blocks stay with that foreground. Mandatory choices and required celebrations block Escape. Dismissal restores focus and preserves the restored view’s pause state. With no overlay open, a fresh Escape opens Menu on Start and Pause during active walking.
- **Navigation** follows the shared [Pause and menu navigation policy](https://github.com/andornaut/game-coordinator/blob/main/docs/design/game-policies.md#pause-and-menu-navigation). The navigation entry shows Pause only when play can be paused and the three-line Menu icon elsewhere. Home shows Sound, Menu and Exit in order. Exit is available only when the platform lets the game close its own app window. In browser tabs and apps the game cannot close, Sound and Menu move to the rightmost columns. Walk, Pause and other two-control bars keep Sound and Pause or Menu. Open Settings from the navigation modal. Sound also appears on the crash screen and stays dimmed and inactive behind a modal. Boot, loading and Retry omit it. M toggles mute.
- **Menus** end with primary Close. The title Menu offers Cheats, Help, Logbook and Settings. The Pet shop and Logbook menus offer those same entries plus Back to title. The Help and Settings page menus offer Cheats and the other of those two, plus Back to title. Cheats opens only from a Menu or Pause, so its page's Menu action returns there. The results menu offers Cheats, Settings and Back to title. Back to title sits just above Close. Other entries are alphabetized and have no visible number prefixes, like Pause. Choice cards keep their numbers.
- **Pausing** stops the road, pack and change labels, and holds the age of message-strip lines. Hints and achievement toasts can still appear. The walk pauses for Pause, Settings, Help, an end-walk confirmation, a card, the neighbourhood celebration, a held explanation, a hidden tab, loss of focus or device rotation. Returning to a hidden tab shows Pause. Pause offers Cheats, Help, Settings, Back to title and primary Resume in one equal-width column below its earnings note. Tab and Shift+Tab cycle through its actions and X. Digits 1 to 5 activate Resume, Help, Settings, Back to title and Cheats.
- **Back and Close** follow the shared [navigation policy](https://github.com/andornaut/game-coordinator/blob/main/docs/design/game-policies.md#back-close-and-resume). Back returns to the view that opened the page. Cheats, Help, Logbook and Settings opened from Menu return to that same Menu through Back or the page's Menu action. Cheats, Help and Settings opened from Pause return to Pause with the walk still paused. Restore focus to the invoking control when available.
  - An eligible **browser or device Back** press dismisses the innermost eligible layer. With nothing dismissible during a walk, it opens Pause, including over a choice card or neighbourhood celebration. On Pause it returns to play, and on End the walk? it cancels the request, returning to the fork card when it asked. On the end screen with nothing open it returns home. On Start with nothing open, it asks “Quit?” with Exit and Cancel. Cancel stays on Start and leaves the next Back press armed. Confirming Exit asks the shared guard to leave once where it can. If leaving is unavailable or fails, the current screen stays. Back does nothing between the end of a walk and the end screen.
- **Panels** use the caller's width family, with Pause and title Menu sharing Medium. Ordinary modals target the viewport's vertical centre and clamp within the safe bounds. Titles centre across the whole panel at full size, wrap at words and grow the panel. Canvas modals fit their content and DOM modals scroll overflow. Dismissible modals have a top-right X separate from their action area. Screen readers announce Close with the modal title when a footer action also says Close. In other modals they announce Close. Required choice cards, including Fork, and required Retry or update recovery keep their no-X layouts while they have no valid cancellation. Cheats, Help, Logbook, the Pet shop and Settings are frameless pages under the live top bar, on the same level as it, with no X. Their Back action and browser or device Back leave the page; a tap outside a page, on the top bar's empty area, the walk bar or beside the game, does nothing. The top bar's controls keep working over every page, and M mutes. Help keeps its framed illustration, caption and fixed page controls while only its body scrolls. A modal above a page dims it and the top bar. All covered content and controls stay laid out, uniformly dimmed and inactive. Notices stay behind the modal and scrim. See [Page bounds and alignment](../../../art/layout.md#page-boundsalignment) and [Modals](../../../art/layout.md#modals).
- **Modal dismissal** leaves one layer and restores focus to the invoking control when available or to a logical target in the restored view. X invokes the same cancellation or dismissal action exactly once. Eligible outside taps use that same one-layer path. Outside includes covered controls and the window's letterbox bars. Empty panel space and drags leave the modal open. The dismissing input cannot select, buy, move the pack or activate another control.
  - A dismissible modal without semantic choices has a centred footer with Close, Back or Resume below its body. Dismissing Pause returns to play.
  - A decision modal has one visible cancellation in its action area. Optional Update, item purchases and the platform Back confirmation use Cancel. End the walk? uses Keep walking, and the separate Quit decision uses Keep playing. Their existing cancellation and save guards remain. Required loading and update recovery stay blocking.
- **Explicit actions** advance choice cards and the full-scene Neighbourhood done and New walker celebrations. An outside tap does nothing on those screens. Choice cards keep the first-second tap guard. Fresh button presses and releases on the end screen and both celebrations work after the first drawn frame. An inherited release does nothing. The celebrations also accept intentional non-repeated Enter, Space or 1 presses. On the end screen with nothing open and no action focused, a fresh Enter, Space or Escape press returns home.
- **Exit:** where Exit shows, Exit and Q on the home screen close the app directly, with no confirmation; Exit acts on release. Where Exit is hidden, Q does nothing

## First-time hints

- **First-time hints:** a one-line hint in the message strip the first time each thing appears (first puddle, first pull, first treat, the first moving obstacle's cue and so on); the help screen covers the rest. The firing hint jumps ahead of any waiting hint, so it shows before the first firing. Nothing teaches by overlaying the road: no tutorial overlays or swipe tips on the play area

## Sound and settings

- **Settings** (a page opened from the Pause or Menu navigation modal): follow the shared [Settings policy](https://github.com/andornaut/game-coordinator/blob/main/docs/design/game-policies.md#settings). Archie's controls include Sound (On or Off, with "Controls music and sound effects"), Music, Sound effects, and Reduced motion (On or Off, with "Skips shakes, bobs, confetti and decorative movers"). The two volume sliders move in 5% steps and show their saved percentage, or Muted while Sound is off; the saved volumes stay unchanged. An Install row, until the game is installed, opens the browser's install prompt or, where the browser has none, shows its steps: on iOS Share, then Add to Home Screen; in Firefox for Android the menu, then More, then Add app to Home screen; in Chromium browsers on Android the menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app; in Samsung Internet the menu, then Add page to, then Home screen; in Chromium browsers on desktop Install in the address bar or the browser menu; in Safari on Mac File, then Add to Dock; in Firefox on desktop that Firefox can't install the game, so open it in Chrome or Edge; and in any other browser its menu, to install it or add it to the home screen. After the first finished walk, the walker's bubble on the next start screen suggests installing, once, except in Firefox on desktop. When it runs installed, never in a browser tab, the game asks the browser to keep its saves. Settings are kept per device, apart from the save, so a save reset leaves them; music volume is 45% until the player changes it on this device, and reduced motion follows the device's setting until the player sets it
  - The **FPS counter** is the last Settings option, below Install when it is available. Its label aligns with the other option labels. Its state below the switch reads Shown or Hidden, because it controls a display; the switch applies immediately and defaults to Hidden. It is a device preference independent of saves. The readout stays at the bottom right of the device viewport, inset from the safe area and above all layers, including the letterbox bars.
- **Sound:** every bump and pickup makes a sound. Short effects (tip, treat, bump, bark, card open, fired, a dodge, a gear find or swap, and the treat's sound again when a spare treat is given back), each lucky find's own sound, a sound for each card obstacle's hit (spray hiss, coin scatter, friendly woof), a splash on a puddle, a bark when a dog spots a squirrel and strains, and a sound of its own when the pull lands (a strain with no pull only barks), the heart-lost sound once after a card choice that costs any owner a heart once forgiveness is counted (a loss Second chance forgives plays nothing), a neighbourhood-done sound with Neighbourhood done (New walker keeps the badge chime), a badge-earned jingle and a new-best fanfare, and a music loop for each neighbourhood, which starts with its banner, cross-fading from the last over about a second, and plays on through its celebration and the fork. While the walk is paused by the player (the pause menu, Settings, Help or the End the walk? question), the music drops to 30% volume and comes back on resume; it stays full under cards and the celebration, and goes silent while the tab is hidden. On iOS the game follows the silent switch: in silent mode it plays no sound

## Loading and updates

- **Loading** uses one strip at the physical top of the viewport, outside the game frame, for startup, Boot, home and walk loading. Boot keeps its decorative artwork behind this same strip. The strip hides while idle and the neighbourhood progress line stays separate. Known file or task progress fills left to right, holds full for 250 ms and fades over 150 ms. An unknown total shows a repeating segment that disappears as soon as the wait ends. Both behaviours apply with reduced motion. The strip shows only for a wait that blocks play, including boot, entering an area, first install, an accepted update or newer-save recovery. First install downloads and verifies the game before offline play. If offline storage is refused or full, the game plays from the network and the walker's bubble says so. See [HUD and overlays](../../../art/layout.md#hudoverlays).
- **Loading errors:** if a required native font or UI asset fails to load, normal game UI stays blocked and a minimal loading-error view offers Retry. Retrying repeats loading, and the game opens only once all required files are ready. If the required WOFF font is unavailable, an independent bootstrap font may render the error and Retry control. Optional art keeps its existing placeholder behaviour. A first install that fails to download uses the same error and Retry view, each Retry press requesting one install retry; one the browser refuses, or that runs out of storage, plays from the network instead (see Loading above).
- **Update notice:** under the shared [Updates policy](https://github.com/andornaut/game-coordinator/blob/main/docs/design/game-policies.md#updates), updates download in the background, fetching only the files that changed, and wait apart from the installed version until complete. At launch, a complete waiting update switches in silently before saves or gameplay start. Boot handles initial discovery, a fresh entry to Start after boot requests an ordinary background update check, and resuming Start from an overlay or page keeps the existing check schedule. When one finishes during a session, a modal reading "Update available" with Cancel and a green Update now button shows wherever the player is, pausing a walk while it shows. Update now saves, then switches to the new version, showing the travelling loading strip while it does; a blocked or failed attempt clears the strip and returns the modal with the title "The update could not finish. Try again.", wrapping at full font size with the header growing to fit. Cancel, the X or a tap outside dismisses it and play continues on the current version; the next launch switches silently. Only Update now switches a running game, including during a walk, a title return or the game coming back into view

The shared [Updates policy](https://github.com/andornaut/game-coordinator/blob/main/docs/design/game-policies.md#updates) governs ordinary updates and the newer-save recovery exception. A save from a newer game is left untouched: Start walk is disabled, incompatible progress is not loaded into play and save writes stay fenced while the required update downloads and activates automatically. The travelling loading bar shows during its download. The launch fence runs before save initialization or game construction; play and save writes resume only after the new document proves compatibility, staying fenced with stored saves untouched if it does not. Offline, or when no newer version is available yet, it says "Update needed. It will install when you're online." and tries again on reconnecting and whenever the game comes back into view, updating the same way once it can.

Save compatibility follows the game's runtime release version under the shared [Saves and cheats policy](https://github.com/andornaut/game-coordinator/blob/main/docs/design/game-policies.md#saves-and-cheats), not the save schema's age or a first-release declaration.

## A walk

- A walk is 4 neighbourhoods of Toronto. Each neighbourhood is the same distance; the first takes about 2½ minutes and later ones less as speed rises. See the [walk duration target](tuning.md#release-and-tuning).
- After neighbourhoods 1 to 3, the [neighbourhood celebration](#neighbourhood-celebration), then a [fork](neighbourhoods.md#forks). Neighbourhood 4 has no fork

## End walk

- The walk ends after neighbourhood 4, on a confirmed early end, or when no dogs are left. What it banks depends on how it ends (see [Cash](economy.md#cash))
- **Interrupted walk:** the game saves the walk at each fork, as each neighbourhood starts, and whenever it's hidden. A walk closed before it ends resumes on the next launch at the start of the neighbourhood it was in, or at the fork it was waiting at, opening straight into the walk with the pause menu up; whatever happened in that neighbourhood before the close is undone and plays again. A saved walk that can't be resumed (it names a dog or neighbourhood the game no longer has, or the save is damaged) is banked as one that headed home
- **End walk:** Back to title from Pause or a navigation Menu during a walk asks "End the walk?". Accepting uses the existing early-end settlement and normal save path before returning directly to the title screen; cancelling keeps the walk active, and a later normal finish still shows the end screen. The fork card's End walk asks the same question and still leads to the end screen. The pause menu's note and the question show how much of the cash ending keeps ("Ending now keeps $45 of $70", or "Ending now keeps all $70" when it keeps it all); under the question, when ending gives anything up, what it gives up in red: what the current neighbourhood has earned (tips, lucky finds and treat cash, less what it has spent; none at a fork); then the number of neighbourhoods left after this one when any are. Keep walking is the default (key 1); End walk is key 2. See [Cash](economy.md#cash) for settlement.
- **End screen:** completed walks, walks ended by the last firing and the fork card's confirmed End walk show the end screen; a confirmed navigation Back to title uses the normal save path and returns directly to the title instead. The end screen shows the walk's total with a breakdown (pay, tips, lucky finds, Walk bonus, card costs, and in red the cash lost when the walk ended mid-neighbourhood), every row shown and a zero reading "$0" with no sign and the neighbourhoods walked, with no bank; a new-best ribbon when it beats the best walk with the same number of dogs; the badges the walk earned, each opening its badge card on a tap or a hold, in one row up to 7 and two rows past that; one closing line from each owner (a happier line from owners still walking, the fired line from owners who fired you), each owner's full name under their face, wrapped only at a space, and a single word too wide for that column shown whole under the bubble. The results panel fits below the returning group. The badge panel centres in the remaining space above Back to title. See [Groups and columns](../../../art/layout.md#groupscolumns).

## Neighbourhood celebration

After each of neighbourhoods 1 to 3, before the fork, a short celebration plays over the paused road. After neighbourhood 4 the end screen takes its place, preceded by a New walker celebration for each walker finishing the walk unlocks.

- **Neighbourhood banner:** each neighbourhood opens with a banner over the road naming the world, the neighbourhood's number and its name; the player never sees the word "zone"
- **Neighbourhood done:** the walker you're walking with cheers, under a big title and the neighbourhood's name, with the pay it banks and its tips, leaving out a part that is $0. It waits for its Onward! button or an intentional non-repeated Enter, Space or 1 press; arbitrary canvas, page and outside taps do not advance it
- **New walker:** the first time you finish a walk that unlocks a walker (see [Walkers](pack.md#walkers)), that walker runs in to join before the end screen, with their greeting, agility and perk, and waits for its Welcome! button or an intentional non-repeated Enter, Space or 1 press. Mr. B looks down at his phone, missing the celebration around him. When one walk unlocks several walkers, each joins in turn, in unlock order
- Confetti plays in both; with reduced motion it doesn't

## Feedback

- **Every change shows its cause:** each ♥ change shows its amount over the dog and on its doghouse card ("−1♥"), with its cause in the message strip, and each $ change a label with its amount alone ("+$1", "−$20"; see Heart changes and Money changes below); a card's enlarged view lists the modifiers behind its numbers
- **Bump flash:** non-dodged bumps and contact obstacles or movers, including puddles, potholes and contacts whose effects are prevented, share one 280 ms feedback sequence. An opaque 5 px border with a 1 px inner ink keyline and a 12% contact-colour road tint show during 0–80 ms and 120–200 ms. The road offsets (+5, +5) px in the first window, returns to zero during 80–120 ms, then offsets (−5, −5) px in the second window. During 200–280 ms the border and tint are off while the road settles linearly to zero at whole-pixel offsets. Only the border promotes amber and tan to yellow, cream and light grey to white, and mauve to pink; other vivid colours stay unchanged. Reduced motion shows one static 280 ms border and tint, without repeated flashing or displacement. A new hit resets and restarts the sequence; leaving the scene clears it. Contact-obstacle and contact-mover splashes and debris appear below actors, including for prevented effects. Contacts of the same obstacle kind share one feedback event per rendered frame, including across multiple fixed steps. Material sound plays at most once per cue per 100 ms across the pack
- **Debuff contact cues:** when an obstacle or mover contact actually applies or refreshes Muddy, Spotlight, Stinky or Zoomies, one native 60×36 px cue displayed at 2× (120×72 px) in both motion modes follows the feet of the dog leading the formation when the cue begins, above ground shadows and below all actors and leashes. Each cue uses the exact colour of its effect's progress bar, plays twelve 100 ms frames once for 1,200 ms without repeating, and is clipped to the road rather than moved away from the feet. Reduced motion holds peak frame 4 for the full 1,200 ms. The matching native 16×16 px HUD icon appears at 2× (32×32 px), without a plate, with its bottom 4 px above the lead dog's opaque top. Its position is captured once and kept inside the viewport; it neither follows the pack nor rises, and fades away over 1,000 ms in both motion modes. Distinct debuff icons sit without overlap, with 4 px gaps; another popup of the same kind replaces its existing icon. One sound for each acquired or refreshed effect kind plays at onset through the existing mute and volume controls; actual applications or refreshes of the same debuff kind share one ground cue, icon popup and sound per rendered frame across multiple fixed steps, with no duplicate sounds per pack member. Distinct debuff kinds and applications in the next frame can produce their own cues; coalescing feedback does not change the applied effects. Prevented effects and card outcomes produce no debuff-acquisition ground cue, icon popup or matching effect sound; physical material audio remains separate
- **Effect icons:** upgrades in effect, the walker's perk, the Pocket item and found gear show as small icons in the walk bar's wells. Tap or hold one to see what it does. An effect that only makes more tips or treats appear (Keen eye, Rush hour) pops no icon on the road when one is grabbed, and neither does Long reach: a tip or treat it reaches shows a dotted line from the dog and a glint on its well, and its icon rides on the gain's chip, at the chip's left: a tip's "+$1" (a merged tip chip keeps it when any of its tips was reached), a treat's "+1♥", where it flies into the dog's card beside the heart, or the cash a treat pays when every owner is full
- **Firing moment:** when an owner fires you, their face pops up large just above the dog's doghouse card, never over it, with their fired line, a "FIRED!" plate shows, and the dog trots off the road edge. It plays right after the hit or card choice that fired you, and the walk holds still for about 3 seconds while it plays: the road, the pack and every mover stand still, and the face, plate, fired line and trot-off all end with the hold
- **Forgiven heart:** whenever Second chance forgives a heart, from a bump or a card choice, a label floats over the dog (like "Dodge!"), the heart on its doghouse card flashes back with a sparkle, and the message strip says so
- **Heart changes:** a lost heart pops over the dog on the road as a 32 px broken heart beside a chip showing the signed numeric change at 2×, without a duplicate heart glyph, then arcs down into its doghouse card, where a label shows the amount alone ("−1♥"); the message strip names the cause. The chip has 4 px padding and sits 4 px to the heart's right; the whole pair stays inside the viewport with the number on the right. A gained heart pops in pink and flies up from the dog the same way. Every heart change plays this, whatever its cause
- **Hit moments:** every card obstacle's hit plays a big moment before its card: a burst over the pack, a brief flash at the screen edges, a large hit label over the pack, kept on screen, and the pack shaking off
- **Last heart:** when an owner drops to 1♥, a "{owner} has 1♥ left!" label rises over their doghouse card, and the card's rim pulses while they stay at 1♥
- **Message strip:** a strip over the clouds at the top of the backdrop band tells what just happened, one line at a time with its icon: icon-led lines use up to 2 rows; owner-led lines retain every measured row at full size. See the [message strip priority rule](#rule-for-message-strip-priority) for timing and waiting lines. It has a line for: a badge earned, a bump, a debuff starting and ending, a dodge, a dog's size changing lane speed (as the walk starts), an effect starting (the effect line of an upgrade, the walker's perk or the Pocket item), a firing, first-time hints (see [First-time hints](#first-time-hints)), a forgiven heart, a gear find or swap, a lucky find, an owner's quip (after a card choice or a lucky find), a pull's strain, a spare treat kept or spent, a treat, a treat that pays cash, and the Walk bonus. An owner's quip is styled like the other lines, led by the owner's face with their full name on one Body 2× line under it in place of the icon. The lead column grows to the wider of the 22 px framed face and the name's visible ink, reducing the quip's room accordingly; the panel grows to fit the complete lead and wrapped quip. Its text has no name prefix
- **Money changes:** a money label shows only the amount, with no cause or modifier text
  - **Gains:** a gain's label, a tip's (a Stinky tip's red "+$0" included), a lucky find's, a treat's cash or a card option's, starts just by the cash total in the top bar and moves into it, so no gain's label covers the road; two at once stack one under the other. Tips grabbed within about a second of each other share one label that adds up ("+$2"); another gain never adds up with a tip
  - **Pay:** at a neighbourhood's end each dog's pay coin flies from its doghouse card to the cash, and one chip by the cash shows the neighbourhood's total pay, the Walk bonus included, until the last coin lands
  - **Losses:** a loss's label pops where the money changed hands, over the pack for a card option or a Spotlight bump, then flies up into the cash total, the way a heart flies into its doghouse card
- **Owner faces:** an owner at full hearts looks happy, whatever their max. Below full, an owner looks upset at 1♥, neutral at 2♥ or when more than one heart below their max, and happy otherwise (one below for an owner with more than 3♥)
- **Return home:** the end screen shows the walker and pack coming back up the front path while the owners wave; fired dogs are absent
- **Road markers:** things worth grabbing (tips, treats and lucky finds) bob gently; obstacles carry no marker. With reduced motion nothing bobs
- **Tip grabbed:** an amber coin sparkle twinkles once, briefly, just above the lead dog's head (above the walker's when no dog is shown), and is a tip's only sparkle; tips and cash treats grabbed together restart it rather than adding more. With reduced motion it doesn't play
- **Treat grabbed:** the owner smiles and the heart change plays (see Heart changes). A treat that pays cash instead plays the tip's coin sparkle, as its only sparkle. The Lost wallet, neighbourhood pay and the Walk bonus keep their own animations and play no coin sparkle

### Rule for message strip priority

Each line shows for at least about 1.5 seconds. Queued badge lines, hints and the firing line wait until shown. A regular waiting line drops after about 3 seconds. If the waiting list fills, the oldest regular line drops.

Evidence: [implementation](../../../src/hud/messageStrip.ts), [tests](../../../src/hud/messageStrip.test.ts), and `adw-0070`.
