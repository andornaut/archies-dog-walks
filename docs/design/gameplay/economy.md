# Economy

Walk earnings, purchases and permanent progression.

## Cash

- **Money ($):** the only currency, in whole dollars. A multiplied amount rounds to the nearest dollar
  - **Cash:** what you carry during the walk. It starts at $0 and grows with tips and lucky finds; card choices and [Spotlight bumps](encounters.md#encounters-and-debuffs) are the only ways to spend it during the walk. It never goes below $0. What the walk banks depends on how it ends: a finished walk banks all its cash; heading home, or every owner firing you, banks the cash held at the end of the last finished neighbourhood, losing what the current one has earned since. Card options spend the current neighbourhood's earnings first, then the earlier cash, so a walk whose card costs spent into what it would keep keeps what's left. The end screen shows what a walk lost as a Cash lost line. Settling a walk never reduces the saved bank balance, whether the walk finishes, ends early, loses every dog, or is interrupted and cannot resume
  - **Tips:** each tip pays $1, regardless of pack size. The request count falls by three each neighbourhood, from 31 to 22 before local density scaling. Kensington Market requests a fifth more.
  - **Pay:** the steady part. After each neighbourhood, each walking dog earns $20, the same in every neighbourhood. Pay joins the cash at the end of each neighbourhood (at its celebration, or on the end screen after neighbourhood 4); the cash held then is what an early end keeps. A firing loses that dog's pay for the current neighbourhood and the Walk bonus; its earlier pay stays in the cash
- **Walk bonus:** when every dog you set out with gets home at the end of neighbourhood 4, the walk pays a Walk bonus of $50 into the cash, whatever the pack size, shown on the end screen. Heading home early or a firing loses it

## Start and picker

Bought dogs, dog slots and gear are permanent. Upgrade cards are walk-only boosts. Progress (bank, owned items, stats, achievements) is saved between sessions; storage is in [`src/README.md`](../../../src/README.md).

- **Home layout** puts the bank and controls above the gate scene. The bank centres the dollar sign and amount together at full size, with room for five characters including the sign. An amount that does not fit rounds down in short form to at most three digits, with one decimal under ten of its unit ("$10k", "$123k", "$1.2M", "$12M", "$1.2B"). The three contiguous pickers follow the gate divider in order, headed Pick a walker, Pick your dogs and Pick your gear (Pocket, optional), then Start walk. Centre card grids using their edges and keep badge overhang clear of the frame. Empty sections keep distinct neutral headers and matching backings, with white text. A pick changes the section to amber with ink text and lights its frame. An empty Pocket stays neutral. The Dogs band shows the pack count with no label ("1/2"). Dogs appear in one row per size from small to large, cheapest first, with Archie in the middle of his row at the centre of the grid. See [Groups and columns](../../../art/layout.md#groupscolumns).
- **Gate scene** shows the chosen walker and pack on the front path above the divider, under the "Toronto" street sign and beside the "Archie's Dog Walks" yard sign and Pet shop. The Pocket badge sits beside the walker. On a new save, the starter's owner waits at the gate until the first walk, and Casey and Archie are already picked. Keep the ground under the pack clear.
- **Tile states** (walkers, dogs and Pocket gear alike, each state plainly distinct from the others at a glance, and the same in all three rows): every tile shows its real picture, never a silhouette. For sale (not owned, on the start screen and in the Pet shop alike): a grey tile or row; when the bank covers it, its picture shows in full colour with a green dollar bill and a green buy plate with the price, and when it doesn't, the picture is greyed with a closed padlock and a grey plate with the price in red; Locked walker: the picture greyed, with a closed padlock and its goal plate; Available (owned, not picked): full colour on a cream tile in a plain frame; Picked: full colour on a gold face with a check and selected lift; Pack full (owned, not picked, while every slot is filled): greyed in colour, and a tap says the pack is full. See [Selection controls](../../../art/controls.md#selection-controls).
- **Details** open on hold for walkers, dogs and gear on Start, and for dogs, gear and slots in the Pet shop. Walker details show lane-change speed, perk and any unlock goal. Dog details show breed, size, hearts and the owner's portrait, name and profession. Walk stats stay in the Logbook's Dogs tab. Gear details show the item's name and effect. Slot details show its capacity.
- **Start walk** is the only button below the pickers and retains its label in every state. It requires a walker and at least one dog. Pocket is optional.
- **Walker picker:** choose from the walkers you've unlocked. Walkers, dogs and the Pocket item share one pick toggle: tap to pick, tap the picked one again to unpick it. The walker, dogs and Pocket item picked are saved and come back picked; one unpicked stays unpicked. Every walker tile, owned or locked, carries the walker's name on a grey plate; locked walkers use the Locked walker tile state; tapping one shakes it and the bubble gives the goal
- **Walker's bubble:** every home screen message shows in the walker's speech bubble (see [Voice](player-copy.md#voice) for lengths). It reacts to state: the first visit (how to pick and hold), after each of the first 3 walks (how to pick and hold), after later walks, after a firing, when something new in the shop is affordable, the one-time install suggestion (see [Sound and settings](rules.md#sound-and-settings)), with no walker picked, a picked walker's agility and perk, and a newly unlocked walker, which comes ahead of the firing line. It also shows buying a dog (with the owner's parting line), what more you need to save for something you can't afford, a full pack, sound on or off, and notices: unsaved progress, a pending update and playing online when the game couldn't be stored for offline play. A notice shows alone, in place of the message it would stack on, so the bubble keeps clear of the walker. After a walk that unlocks several walkers, the bubble names the first; each tap on the bubble or the walker at the gate names the next, in unlock order, then shows the walker's usual line, then starts again, and a tap while a message or an owner's line shows brings the walker's line back
- **Owner parting line:** picking a dog shows a line from its owner in the walker's bubble, with the owner's face on the right and their name under it

## Pet shop and purchases

- **Pet shop:** a building in the gate scene, its sign the same green as Start walk (tappable entry points share one colour); tap it to browse gear, dogs and dog slots. It has two tabs, Dogs on the left and open by default, then Gear; the selected tab stays static
  - **Dogs tab:** lists all 9 dogs, cheapest first, each row showing its size and hearts and marking owned ones as Gear does, and all 3 dog slots in order, the starting one included, owned ones marked as for dogs and gear (each one holds 1 dog; the first is free, the second costs $1,000 and the third $3,000; slots can be bought in any order, and the pack holds one dog per slot owned; one for sale looks like a dog or gear item for sale)
  - **Gear tab:** lists the 5 items, each with a short effect line, marking owned ones
  - **Headings and rows** show ownership counts for Gear, Dogs and Slots ("1 of 9 owned"). Rows for sale follow the For sale tile state. Tap an affordable row to open its item panel. Tapping an owned row focuses it. Unaffordable or disabled rows receive no cyan selection cue from taps, keyboard input or entry from Start. Existing keyboard actions and hold for details still apply. An unaffordable row gives refusal feedback and shows how much more you need.
  - **Shop entrance** gets a gold outline while any item is affordable. While the home screen is idle, the door opens, the shopkeeper waves and the door closes for a pause, regardless of affordability. Reduced motion keeps the door closed. Pressing the shop or opening a panel cancels and resets the animation.
  - **Row focus** uses an interior cue. Availability stays visible, including grey art, a padlock and a red price when unaffordable. See [Selection controls](../../../art/controls.md#selection-controls).
- **Dog slot sign:** a small sign hanging from the Pet shop's awning, across its front, reading "Walk more dogs!" over the price of the cheapest slot for sale (ties to the lower number). It shows while a slot is for sale and gets the Pet shop's gold outline when the bank covers that slot. Tap it to open the affordable slot's item panel. An unaffordable slot opens the Dogs tab without selecting the slot.
- **Item purchases** use the same detail panel on Start and in the Pet shop. Tap an affordable dog, gear item or slot entry to open it directly. On Start, tapping an unaffordable item opens its shop tab without selecting the item. For an affordable item, the panel shows the price, with Cancel and primary Buy. Buy rechecks eligibility and affordability before completing the purchase. Your current picks stay as they were. Owned or unavailable item details use Close. If you hold an unaffordable item to inspect it, its panel centres the price and "Save up for it!" on separate lines.

## Gear and Pocket

- **Gear and Pocket** (5 items, bought once, priced by how much each helps: Fanny pack, then Hall pass, then Running shoes, then Lucky collar, then Whistle):
  - **Fanny pack:** keeps one spare treat: a treat grabbed while every walking owner is at their max goes into the pack instead of paying its cash, and the moment any owner loses a heart, the spare treat gives it back; when several lose one at once, it goes to the owner left with the fewest hearts, ties to the lead dog; Second chance's forgiveness comes first. Its walk bar well shows whether a treat is kept, and a spent treat flies from the well to the dog's doghouse card
  - **Hall pass:** once per neighbourhood, skip one encounter card for free. See [Choice cards](encounters.md#choice-cards).
  - **Lucky collar:** every owner has +1 max ♥
  - **Running shoes:** lane changes +100%
  - **Whistle:** medium and large dogs also dodge 1 in 5 of the bumps that would land on them, as small dogs do
  - **Pocket:** put 1 owned item in your Pocket before each walk, or none. The Pocket section is a strip of all 5 items: tap an owned item to pocket it (gold frame), tap it again to empty the Pocket; an unowned item shows its price and follows the [item purchase rules](#pet-shop-and-purchases). The section's band names the pocketed item, or says one per walk when the Pocket is empty; hold an item for its effect. The item is kept, never used up, and switches freely between walks; its effects refresh each walk

## Logbook and achievements

- **Logbook** (the home Menu's Logbook action opens it directly on the Badges tab, with no card in between; the screens say "walk", never "run"): the Walks tab lists walks started; walks finished, headed home or out of dogs, under How walks ended; dogs walked, across all walks; the most earned in one walk, with that walk's neighbourhoods and dogs; lifetime earnings; total firings and the owner who fired you most; most neighbourhoods finished in one walk (heading home mid-neighbourhood doesn't count it). Every tab is one column on the same cream panel in the same text size, and scrolls when it runs long. No heading repeats the open tab's name. The Dogs tab lists one block per dog: its face and name, then its walks, neighbourhoods finished and times fired, one per row
- **Achievements** (the badges are in [`src/data/achievements.ts`](../../../src/data/achievements.ts), ordered Easy, Medium, then Hard in the Logbook, results and badge notifications, retaining their catalogue order within each tier regardless of earned status): the Badges tab of the Logbook, one badge per row with its name, goal and progress, earned badges in colour on a gold band with a tick, and locked ones greyed; a locked badge that counts across walks also shows its progress ("7/75"). Badges come in about equal thirds for a typical player: easy ones within a few walks, medium ones within 10 to 30, and hard ones taking 40 or more, so the full set is still the last thing a player completes, after buying out the Pet shop. A badge that counts bumps counts every bump that lands, forgiven ones included, never a dodged one, the same as the bump-free badges. Each badge shows its name, difficulty, goal and one line. Earning one plays a short celebration (owners cheer) wherever it happens; there is no other reward. A money goal counts the walk's total on the end screen, checked when it's banked. A streak badge counts walks in a row; a firing ends the streak, and heading home early neither ends it nor adds to it. A badge for a card option counts each time that option is picked, across walks
