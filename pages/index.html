Walk a pack of dogs through Toronto. Every card obstacle turns into a choice.

A cozy pixel-art lane runner for your phone. Four neighbourhoods a walk, about 9 minutes.

Play

About

  • Pick a walker, your dogs and one gear item for your Pocket, then head out.
  • Dodge bumps, collect tips and treats, and choose how to handle each card obstacle.
  • Spend what you earn at the Pet shop on new dogs, dog slots and gear.

Controls

Walking
Action Input
Steer Left/Right arrows, A/D or H/L. Tap either half of the road, or drag left/right.
Choose a card Press its number, or click/tap the card.
Pause or resume P or Escape, or use Pause/Resume.
Sound M, or click/tap the sound button.
Menus and loadout
Action Input
Move focus Arrow keys, W/A/S/D, H/J/K/L or Tab. Shift+Tab moves backwards.
Choose or confirm Enter, Space, E or F. Click/tap a control, or right-click an available action.
Item details Press and hold a walker, dog, gear item or Pet shop row.
Back or close Escape, or Back/Close/X, when dismissal is allowed.
Volume Left/Right arrows on a focused slider, or click/tap or drag the slider.
Help Shift+/ (?) on Start, or Help in Menu/Pause.
Exit Q on Start when Exit is shown.
