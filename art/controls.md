# Controls

The normal-form specification for controls across the application: the Phaser game, website, data browser, developer panel, crash screen and art gallery. [Gameplay design](../docs/design/gameplay/design.md) is canonical for gameplay semantics and player wording. The [style guide](style-guide.md) owns the wider art direction, and the [inventory](inventory.md) owns exported asset sizes and tags.

## Scope and exceptions

All surfaces share button roles, palette, borders, raised and pressed feedback, readable disabled states and visible keyboard focus. The Phaser game uses canvas and native DOM controls. Settings mounts in the native DOM, and the website and tools use responsive DOM layouts. Selection controls, colour swatches, switches and sliders preserve their content and semantics instead of becoming ordinary action buttons.

| Control                       | Reason for exception                                             | Rules                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ----------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Choice cards                  | Tall illustrated choices carry art, prices and consequences      | Preserve card geometry, readable text and selection feedback; tap release chooses, a hold enlarges, and outside taps never choose                                                                                              |
| Debuff bars                   | A hold explains the debuff; a bar has no tap action              | Exempt from the 48px size rule: each native debuff bar keeps its 360×24 hold target under the walk bar                                                                                                                         |
| Hold to explain               | A still hold requests details rather than activation             | Keep hold and tap-release distinct; pause the walk for its detail card and consume the dismissing tap                                                                                                                          |
| Effect wells                  | Six effects must fit beside the cash well and two buttons        | Exempt from the 48px size rule: tap to explain keeps each 26×26 HUD well's press, its column of the bar, rows 0–39                                                                                                             |
| Home and walk top bars        | Buttons and cash/effect wells share a 360px-wide, 40px-tall bar  | 20×20 navy faces 24px apart, each with a 48×48 press centred on it on rows 0–47, hanging 8 rows under the bar; overlaps go to the nearest centre; a visible debuff bar wins rows 40–47; 16×16 glyphs, 2px press; no second row |
| Joystick                      | Not applicable: this game uses lane gestures and keys            | No joystick asset, state or input contract is defined                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Lane steering                 | Road input is a movement gesture, not a button                   | Immediate road taps, release-classified doghouse-row taps, lane drags and the lane keys steer; top-bar taps never steer                                                                                                        |
| Loading recovery              | Game resources may be unavailable                                | Use the [resource-independent recovery role](#asset-and-code-contract): fixed CSS panel/text with a caller-fitted, content-width Retry action                                                                                  |
| Pet shop                      | The building and its board form one bespoke control              | The building's visible part, above the divider strip (rows 54–137), is the press area, with its green board and press feedback; never a compact button                                                                         |
| Scrolling pages               | Shop, Logbook, Settings and Cheats lists need a bounded viewport | Keep their page/list geometry and scrolling; ordinary panels opened inside them use the modal contract                                                                                                                         |
| Settings switches and sliders | A small indicator is activated through its whole labelled row    | Keep the row hit area, labels, keyboard behaviour and non-colour state cues                                                                                                                                                    |

Use the wide token for a screen's main text action and the compact token for secondary or paired text actions outside content-sized modals. Content-sized modal buttons, the Help pager's buttons and the pages' Back (`page.back` in the [layout guide](layout.md#page-boundsalignment)) instead use the intrinsic-width contract below. Every button group follows the button gaps below. DOM controls keep responsive widths and a minimum 48px target while sharing the role colours, border/lip and 2px depression. Preserve the same text size; grow the available room, wrap where the role allows, or change wording in the text data when necessary.

**Button gaps.** Gaps are measured between neighbouring faces, in game pixels. They come from game-kit's `ui/spacing` through `BUTTON_SIZE` in [`layout.ts`](../src/data/layout.ts), so every game spaces its buttons alike. Wide, compact (128×48) and content-sized modal buttons are standard; a smaller face is a small button.

| Arrangement            | Gap | Token                  | Applies to                                                                            |
| ---------------------- | --- | ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Side by side, standard | 16  | `BUTTON_SIZE.gap`      | Pairs, modal action rows and the Cheats columns                                       |
| Small, any direction   | 24  | `BUTTON_SIZE.smallGap` | Top-bar faces, Shop and Logbook tabs                                                  |
| Stacked, standard      | 8   | `BUTTON_SIZE.stackGap` | Full-width list rows (badge wall, Pet shop, settings), stacked buttons and tile grids |

The strip's first row is a rule in its plate's edge colour, the rest its plate's face, with the full-size 2x label centred on its ink there. The strip covers at most the picture's bottom 3 rows (walker) or 1 row (gear). Owned gear has no strip.

- **Walk effect wells:** 0px apart; six 26px wells at 24px apart need 276px of the 162px region between the cash well and Sound. For `n` visible wells use width `26n` with no empty reserved wells; centre the group within the fixed region `[102,264)`, centred on x = 183, which stays pinned whatever Sound's press does. Six wells start at x = 105,131,157,183,209,235; two start at x = 157,183. Wells start at y = 5 and icons at y = 6. Sound and Pause/Menu have 20×20 faces at x = 282/326, y = 8, 24px apart with no backing, and 48×48 presses centred on them at x = 268/312. Preserve 3× full-size cash text and the dollar sign; five-character labels such as `$9.9K` and `$999M` fit without changing the well or control positions.

**Press areas.** Every button face is at least 48px on its shorter side, except the small faces (icon faces and tabs), and every press area is at least 48×48 (`press.min`), the face grown about its centre, apart from the debuff bars and effect wells in the scope table. Where neighbouring press areas overlap, the press goes to the control whose drawn centre is nearest (game-kit's `pressedArea`); check non-overlap only against unrelated controls. In a stack the effective press area is the face plus half of each gap. The start-screen top-bar presses hang 8 rows under the 40px bar, over the yard: the bar wins rows 40–47 over the walker's bubble, and the Pet shop and its slot sign start below them. The slot sign's press wins over the Pet shop's where they overlap. The walk bar's presses take rows 0–47; while a debuff bar shows, the debuff bar wins rows 40–47, and with none showing the full 48 rows press the button.

**Canvas modal sizing.** Measure present header, body, actions, icons and illustrations at their full font roles. Select the client family through [`modalFamilyWidth`](../src/ui/modal/layout.ts) and `MODAL_WIDTHS` in [`layout.ts`](../src/data/layout.ts): Large is 328px and Medium 264px on the 360px viewport, clamped to the safe available width. Keep 16px outer margins and body padding, yielding 296px and 232px body rooms. Large serves rich portrait/owner details and ordinary installation cards. Medium serves Pause/Menu, brief confirmations, optional Update and Purchase. The new-badges celebration panel takes Large; the new-walker bubble and the neighbourhood earnings plate take Medium, centred, with height following content. Short gear/icon details use Medium only when the actual full-size wrapped body, heading and complete footer fit; otherwise [`detailCardView`](../src/home/details/detailCardView.ts) selects Large. Derive label wrapping and supported action layouts from the selected body room. Heights remain content-driven; keep complete labels and never shrink fonts. The frameless pages (Shop, Logbook, Help, Settings and Cheats) and illustrated choice cards retain their named geometry.

**Modal placement.** Every ordinary modal follows the one [placement rule](layout.md#modals): target the full viewport's midpoint, clamp to the caller's protected area, and avoid actors horizontally only. Full-scene celebrations and results, the frameless pages and illustrated choice cards keep their own placement; celebration panels take their width family.

**Notice priority.** Gameplay notices stay beneath all overlay scenes and scrims. The message strip is in `HudScene` at `HUD_DEPTH.message - 1`, not in the active pause, settings or card scene, and keeps its paused age and enlarged-card hiding.

Use `modal.gap.rows` between text rows and `modal.gap.groups` between present header/body/action groups. Omit missing groups and their gaps. Use `button.gap` between neighbouring actions and `button.gap.stack` between rows. For ordinary modal actions, measure each full-size label at `max(48, ceil(ink + 32))` and use a 48px target height. Side-by-side actions keep their own intrinsic widths. An explicit vertical stack uses the widest intrinsic width throughout. Support wrapped rows where the caller defines them, with Cheats using its [own grid and text-fit contract](layout.md#settings-and-cheats-pages).

**Action footers.** Two actions sit in one row when both full-size labels fit the body room at `button.gap` (16px); otherwise they stack at `button.gap.stack` (8px) with the primary last. In a row the secondary is on the left and the primary on the right. Question panels, the update notice, the detail card and every other decision dialog follow this rule. A sole visible action is centred; hidden actions do not count. Preserve action roles, callbacks and keyboard choices.

**Modal footer actions.** Follow the shared [product consistency policy](https://github.com/andornaut/game-coordinator/blob/main/docs/game-policies.md#product-consistency). Dismissible panels without semantic choice actions expose their existing close or resume callback in a bottom dismissal row; [`dismissFooter`](../src/ui/modal/dismissFooter.ts) reserves an 8px gap (`MODAL.gap`) and 48px target, the detail card's footer included, centres the full-size label and leaves 16px below it. When each decision action dismisses the modal, keep the existing Cancel, Close or Back as the sole dismissal button in the action area, labelled for its purpose and destination. Place X separately at the top right of each modal with a valid cancellation. Route X and permitted Escape and backdrop input through the same one-layer cancellation exactly once. Restore focus to the invoking control when available or to a logical control in the restored view. Preserve existing confirmation guards, callbacks and keyboard defaults. If no valid cancellation exists, record the current actions and dismissal behaviour for an explicit user decision. Keep mandatory choices nondismissible until a cancellation path is approved. X is a cancellation control and may never accept a decision or advance a story choice. Corner X, footer dismissal and body-reading scroll do not trigger page-action swipes; page-action gestures are opt-in in [`questionPanel.ts`](../src/hud/dom/questionPanel.ts).

[`MODAL.titleTop`](../src/ui/modalGeometry.ts) and [`cardTitleRow`](../src/ui/modal/titleRow.ts) keep the existing actual-ink title spacing.

## Refusal

An attempted disabled Help Previous, refused Start walk or home pick, unavailable card choice, Cheats Reset save while nothing is saved, or muted volume slider keeps its disabled chrome and its price, goal or reason. Refusal does not activate the unavailable action or change its value, its explanation, the keyboard/default choices or the tap guards. Grey actionable shop rows keep their shop/detail actions. Native developer DOM controls keep their native disabled semantics and are outside this cue.

Every refusal uses one cue, `REFUSE` in [`refuse.ts`](../src/ui/refuse.ts), on every surface (panel buttons, Cheats, Settings rows, choice cards, Start picker tiles, Start walk, Shop rows and the Help pager), played by [`refusal.ts`](../src/ui/dom/refusal.ts) on DOM controls and on each scene's clock by [`refuse.ts`](../src/ui/refuse.ts) elsewhere:

- **Motion:** the step shake, 320ms in all. The control moves right only, in whole art pixels, through offsets 0, 1, 2, 3, 2, 1, 0 in each 80ms, four times. It never moves left of its place.
- **Colour:** during the red phases, 80–160ms and 240–320ms, the face fills darkRed with a white label (7.34:1). A faced control shows this on its face, over any focus fill (on a picker tile, its name area; on a Settings row, the whole row, with its row icon drawn as a white silhouette); the label never turns red on its own. Every refusing control, the Help pager included, uses these phases and the shake below.
- **Reduced motion:** no shake; the darkRed face with its white label holds for 0–320ms, then the control's prior look returns.
- **Forced colours:** the red phases fill the face (a choice card's band included) with ButtonText and draw its label and icon in ButtonFace, the inverse of the resting face, so a refusal stays distinct from the Highlight cue. Start picker tiles and Pet shop rows, whose focus and press already use that inverse, fill their refusal with Highlight and draw the label, name and price in HighlightText.

## Interaction and accessibility

- Show press feedback at once: `fx-tap` bursts under the finger. A modal opened by the press opens immediately, and the burst finishes on top of it. Text and chrome move together, and a cancelled press does not activate its former target.
- Keep keyboard focus distinct from selection. Start-screen arrow keys move focus and Enter or Space activates. Start walk uses its standard face and label focus cue for both Tab and scene arrow navigation. The slot sign colours its inner text panel on focus and keeps its authored trim, price colouring and affordability glint. In forced colours, the slot sign uses Highlight and HighlightText for the focused interior. Pet shop keyboard focus uses the board's cyan `focused` face while the control is up, and a press shows its forest `pressed` face with white letters, sunk 2px, while the building stays up; neither adds an outline around the board or the building. The pressed face takes precedence over focus. If the focus tag is unavailable, keep the normal board. The code-drawn fallback colours the board interior cyan on focus while up. Settings reveal focused rows. DOM controls retain native Tab behaviour.
- Escape or P closes the current overlay/pause level; M toggles sound. Context-specific shortcuts and browser-back behaviour are in [`keyBindings.ts`](../src/input/keyBindings.ts).
- UI taps activate on release over the matching target; holding invokes that same action once unless an explicit different hold action is defined. A hold that opens details or enlarges a card retains that override, and its release must not act again. Preserve steering, dragging, native text editing and hold-only debuff bars. Do not require hover for information. Native discrete actions share [`actionBinding.ts`](../src/ui/dom/actionBinding.ts), which delegates hold timing and release suppression to the published kit `bindHoldActions` mechanism.
- Modals guard taps. A tap that dismisses a panel is consumed: it must not select, buy, steer or activate content underneath. All background controls, including Sound/Pause/Menu and the HUD, stay painted and laid out but uniformly dimmed and inactive; only the top modal is interactive. Retain existing background cancellation and Back where closeable; frameless pages have no background cancellation. Mandatory choice cards wait for an explicit choice; outside taps do nothing.
- In confirmations, key 1 keeps the walk going or keeps playing, as the rulebook defines; it must not become the destructive alternative. This does not turn choice-card key 1 into an automatic or default choice.
- Essential labels hold 4.5:1 contrast, or 3:1 at 2× and larger; essential icons hold 3:1. Inactive controls are exempt. State also uses text, glyphs, shape or position.
- Reduced motion preserves visible selection and readable static feedback while skipping movement. Sound is supplementary; it cannot be the sole action/state cue.
- Physical-impact jolts follow the [run-screen feedback contract](style-guide.md#run-screen). Move only the rendered road/backdrop; keep HUD, borders, viewport masks, input targets and logical coordinates fixed. Reduced motion uses one static border/tint cue without displacement or repeated flashing.

The game shell supplies an accessible game label and control instructions. Where controls render on canvas, the shell supplies that context for keyboard interaction. Native Settings rows expose labels, roles and control state through the DOM.

DOM buttons keep native Tab and Enter/Space behaviour, visible focus, meaningful labels (no two controls in one dialog share a spoken name: a modal with both a corner X and a footer Close names its X "Close" plus the modal's title, such as "Close install steps", while the footer keeps its visible label), `disabled` where unavailable and `aria-pressed` where they toggle selection. Disabled labels remain readable instead of fading the whole control.

Update the same presenter with `setPresentation` on fit changes, preserving its button and received-state/input guards. Verify very short fits without shrinking text or claiming universal coverage. ## Review checklist

- Check the control's role, semantic state and availability separately, including its readable price, goal or unavailable reason.
- Use a geometry token from this guide or document a concrete exception; verify each press area is at least 48×48 or a named exemption, and clear of unrelated controls, with neighbours resolved by nearest centre. For modals, check the measured content union, consistent padding, absent groups, close-hit clearance and viewport fit with the minimum of half the full logical viewport width clamped to the safe available width.
- Check text at its full role size, including the longest label and the disabled state; verify canonical player wording and Canadian spelling in the text data.
- Check normal, pressed, disabled, selected and keyboard-focus feedback where each is implemented; do not invent absent states. Verify the authored picker checkmark and the shared pick-again-to-clear behaviour.
- Check tap release, hold/detail separation, consumed modal dismissals and safe continuation defaults.
- Check the same control by touch/pointer, keyboard and reduced motion; test only the changed behaviour locally, with the combined gate owned by the release process.
- Use judgement for routine art within the requested scope, with contextual review where useful; obtain explicit user approval only for controversial art changes.
- Keep generator output, code fallback, source layout and this guide in step.

## Out of scope

Native per-control accessibility semantics for canvas controls and further canvas selection states are outside this contract; adding one needs a decision first.
