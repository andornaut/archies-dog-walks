# Controls

The normal-form specification for controls across the application: the Phaser game, website, data browser, developer panel, crash screen and art gallery. [`gameplay/design.md`](../gameplay/design.md) is canonical for gameplay semantics and player wording. The [style guide](style-guide.md) owns the wider art direction, and the [inventory](inventory.md) owns exported asset sizes and tags.

## Scope and exceptions

All surfaces share button roles, palette, borders, raised and pressed feedback, readable disabled states and visible keyboard focus. The Phaser game uses canvas and native DOM controls. Settings mounts in the native DOM, and the website and tools use responsive DOM layouts. Selection controls, colour swatches, switches and sliders preserve their content and semantics instead of becoming ordinary action buttons.

| Control                       | Reason for exception                                            | Rules                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ----------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Choice cards                  | Tall illustrated choices carry art, prices and consequences     | Preserve card geometry, readable text and selection feedback; tap release chooses, a hold enlarges, and outside taps never choose                                        |
| Debuff bars                   | A hold explains the debuff; a bar has no tap action             | Exempt from the 48px size rule: each native debuff bar keeps its 360×24 hold target under the walk bar                                                                   |
| Hold to explain               | A still hold requests details rather than activation            | Keep hold and tap-release distinct; pause the walk for its detail card and consume the dismissing tap                                                                    |
| Effect wells                  | Six effects must fit beside the cash well and two buttons       | Exempt from the 48px size rule: tap to explain keeps each 26×26 HUD well's press, its column of the bar, rows 0–39                                                       |
| Home and walk top bars        | Buttons and cash/effect wells share a 360px-wide, 40px-tall bar | 48×48 presses at a 48px pitch on rows 0–47, hanging 8 rows under the bar; a visible debuff bar wins rows 40–47; 20×20 navy faces, 16×16 glyphs, 2px press; no second row |
| Joystick                      | Not applicable: this game uses lane gestures and keys           | No joystick asset, state or input contract is defined                                                                                                                    |
| Lane steering                 | Road input is a movement gesture, not a button                  | Immediate road taps, release-classified doghouse-row taps, lane drags and the lane keys steer; top-bar taps never steer                                                  |
| Loading recovery              | Game resources may be unavailable                               | Use the [resource-independent recovery role](#asset-and-code-contract): fixed CSS panel/text with a caller-fitted, content-width Retry action                            |
| Pet shop                      | The building and its board form one bespoke control             | The building's visible part, above the divider strip (rows 54–137), is the press area, with its green board and press feedback; never a compact button                   |
| Scrolling pages               | Shop, settings and logbook lists need a bounded viewport        | Keep their page/list geometry and scrolling; ordinary panels opened inside them use the modal contract                                                                   |
| Settings switches and sliders | A small indicator is activated through its whole labelled row   | Keep the row hit area, labels, keyboard behaviour and non-colour state cues                                                                                              |

They come from game-kit's `ui/spacing` through `BUTTON_SIZE` in [`layout.ts`](../src/data/layout.ts), so every game spaces its buttons alike. Wide, compact (128×48) and content-sized modal buttons are standard; a smaller face is a small button.

**Press areas.** Every button face is at least 48px on its shorter side, except the small faces (icon faces, tabs, page dots), and every press area is at least 48×48 (`press.min`), the face grown about its centre, apart from the debuff bars and effect wells in the scope table. In a stack the effective press area is the face plus half of each gap. ## Interaction and accessibility

- Show press feedback at once: `fx-tap` bursts under the finger. A modal opened by the press opens immediately, and the burst finishes on top of it. Text and chrome move together, and a cancelled press does not activate its former target.
- Keep keyboard focus distinct from selection. Start-screen arrow keys move focus and Enter or Space activates. The separate Start focus overlay still uses its [authored geometry](layout.md#controlstargets). Settings reveal focused rows. DOM controls retain native Tab behaviour.
- Escape or P closes the current overlay/pause level; M toggles sound. Context-specific shortcuts and browser-back behaviour are in [`keyBindings.ts`](../src/input/keyBindings.ts).
- UI taps activate on release over the matching target; holding invokes that same action once unless an explicit different hold action is defined. A hold that opens details or enlarges a card retains that override, and its release must not act again. Preserve steering, dragging, native text editing and hold-only debuff bars. Do not require hover for information. Native discrete actions share [`actionBinding.ts`](../src/ui/dom/actionBinding.ts), which delegates hold timing and release suppression to the published kit `bindHoldActions` mechanism.
- Modals guard taps. A tap that dismisses a panel is consumed: it must not select, buy, steer or activate content underneath. All background controls, including Sound/Pause/Menu and the HUD, stay painted and laid out but uniformly dimmed and inactive; only the top modal is interactive. Retain existing background cancellation and Back where closeable. Mandatory choice cards wait for an explicit choice; outside taps do nothing.
- In confirmations, key 1 keeps the walk going or keeps playing, as the rulebook defines; it must not become the destructive alternative. This does not turn choice-card key 1 into an automatic or default choice.
- Essential labels hold 4.5:1 contrast, or 3:1 at 2× and larger; essential icons hold 3:1. Inactive controls are exempt. State also uses text, glyphs, shape or position.
- Reduced motion preserves visible selection and readable static feedback while skipping movement. Sound is supplementary; it cannot be the sole action/state cue.
- Physical-impact jolts follow the [run-screen feedback contract](style-guide.md#run-screen). Move only the rendered road/backdrop; keep HUD, borders, viewport masks, input targets and logical coordinates fixed. Reduced motion uses one static border/tint cue without displacement or repeated flashing.

The game shell supplies an accessible game label and control instructions. Where controls render on canvas, the shell supplies that context for keyboard interaction. Native Settings rows expose labels, roles and control state through the DOM.

DOM buttons keep native Tab and Enter/Space behaviour, visible focus, meaningful labels, `disabled` where unavailable and `aria-pressed` where they toggle selection. Disabled labels remain readable instead of fading the whole control.

## Review checklist

- Check the control's role, semantic state and availability separately, including its readable price, goal or unavailable reason.
- Use a geometry token from this guide or document a concrete exception; verify each press area is at least 48×48 or a named exemption, and clear of unrelated controls, with neighbours resolved by nearest centre. For modals, check the measured content union, consistent padding, absent groups, close-hit clearance and viewport fit with the minimum of half the full logical viewport width clamped to the safe available width.
- Check text at its full role size, including the longest label and the disabled state; verify canonical player wording and Canadian spelling in the text data.
- Check normal, pressed, disabled, selected and keyboard-focus feedback where each is implemented; do not invent absent states. Verify the authored picker checkmark and the shared pick-again-to-clear behaviour.
- Check tap release, hold/detail separation, consumed modal dismissals and safe continuation defaults.
- Check the same control by touch/pointer, keyboard and reduced motion; test only the changed behaviour locally, with the combined gate owned by the release process.
- Use judgement for routine art within the requested scope, with contextual review where useful; obtain explicit user approval only for controversial art changes.
- Keep generator output, code fallback, source layout and this guide in step.
